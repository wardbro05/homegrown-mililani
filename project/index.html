Our Story

WE ARE
HOMEGROWN.

Homegrown Baseball was built on a simple idea: the best players aren't discovered somewhere else — they're developed right here, on the fields of Mililani, by coaches who live and breathe this community.

We don't recruit from the mainland. We don't chase shortcuts. We grow our players from the ground up — with elite instruction, relentless reps, and the kind of aloha spirit that makes Hawaii baseball different from anywhere else on earth.

Whether your kid is picking up a bat for the first time or chasing a D1 scholarship, Homegrown has a place for them.
